# Sheet music and MIDI

- **Sheet music** tab: shows the selected track, or all tracks, as notation. It updates as you edit.
- **Export ▾ → MusicXML**: opens in MuseScore (free) for printing or fine notation work.
- **Export ▾ → MIDI**: for other music apps.
- **Import…** (or drag and drop): MIDI (`.mid`) and MusicXML (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`). Each part becomes a track with a fitting sound; drums go to a drum track. An import is one undo step.
- **A photo or PDF of a score**: give it to Claude and ask it to turn it into tracks. Claude reads the notation and imports it.
