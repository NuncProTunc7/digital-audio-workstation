# Sheet music and MIDI

- **Sheet music** tab: shows the selected track, or all tracks, as notation. It updates as you edit. Chords from the chord track are named above the top line.
- **Guitar tab**: a guitar track shows as guitar music usually looks: the notes on a staff (written an octave up, the small 8 under the clef), with a **tab** under it: six lines, one per string (the high E string at the top), and the fret number to play on each. The frets are where the app's guitar really plays each note (the highest string that reaches it), in your tuning; with a capo, 0 means the capo. While a guitar is on the page, the toolbar switch picks **Notes + tab**, **Tab** only, or **Notes** only. Notes the guitar can't reach in its tuning are left out of the tab.
- **Export ▾ → MusicXML**: opens in MuseScore (free) for printing or fine notation work. Guitar tracks come with their tab, tuning and capo, so MuseScore and Guitar Pro show the tab too.
- **Export ▾ → MIDI**: for other music apps.
- **Import…** (or drag and drop): MIDI (`.mid`) and MusicXML (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`). Each part becomes a track with a fitting sound; drums go to a drum track. An import is one undo step.
- **A photo or PDF of a score**: give it to your AI (one that can read pictures, such as Claude) and ask it to turn it into tracks. It reads the notation and imports it.
